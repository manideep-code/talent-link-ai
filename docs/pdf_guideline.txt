The arrangement of paragraph in a Chapter: 
 
Each main topic Title in a chapter should be properly numbered for example in 
chapter 2, it should be: 2.1, 2.2. etc., (Font: 14, TNR, Bold and Upper case) where, the first 
digit represents the Chapter number, and second digit, the topic Title number in serial order. 
Sub-topic titles, if any, may be indicated as 1.1.1, 1.1.2, etc. (Font: 14, TNR, Bold and 
title case) i.e. the first digit representing the chapter, the second representing the topic title 
and the third representing the sub-topic title. 
Photographs and Tables: 
 
The photographs and tables occurring in a chapter may be serially numbered as Fig: 
1.1, 1.2 etc., where the first digit represents the chapter, the second digit represents figure 
number. 
The photograph may be represented as: Screen 1.1, 1.2 etc., the first digit representing 
chapter and the second representing the photograph number.
